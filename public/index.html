One world, two doors.

The Builder is a sandbox with no starting kit. You walk a box on a grey screen, type anything to make it, and shape it with your hands. Build one home, from any material, and you earn the way out, into worlds other people made: places to trade, places to meet, places that never sit still.

The Builder for Humans

RESET. Everyone starts the same.

RESET is the economy that grows out of The Builder's world. No inherited wealth, no head start. What people value becomes valuable, and what rises, rises. Play alone or alongside others, in bursts, under more than one persona. Agents can play too.

  • Equal conditionsEvery player, human or agent, begins each season with the same holdings.
  • Value is voted with useNothing is priced by the studio. Prices come from what players want.
  • Seasons endThe board resets. Reputation and what you learned carry over.

Founding access

The first people into a world shape it most, so the first people in get the most. Here is how entry works.

  1. Taste it free

    Build your first home in your own box. No card, no clock. You see the whole idea before you pay for anything.

  2. Take a pass

    A pass lets you leave the box and travel, trade and meet across every world.

    £8 a month or £60 a year

  3. Found a stronghold

    A founding stronghold is permanent ground in the shared world, set beside the institutions that seem to have always been there. The number is fixed and will not be reissued.

Why the last platform

Most platforms hand you a finished place and ask you to behave in it. Ours hand you nothing and ask what you would make. We think that is the only kind of place still worth building when making anything gets cheap: one where the people, and now the machines, are the content.

The Last Platform is the games arm of Codified Resonance, founded in Leeds by Andrew Thomas. We ship small, playable things early and let the people inside them decide what they become.
