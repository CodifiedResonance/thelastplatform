The Last Platform

Where worlds emerge.

Every platform before this one was a way to deliver finished things to people. This is the one at the end of that line. Worlds emerge here, for people and for machines, each on its own terms. What comes after is not known.

More will emerge here. They will not be announced.

How it holds

  • Each world keeps its own name, its own mark and its own door. The platform frames a world and never restyles it.
  • Each world carries its own terms: how it is entered, how it is played, what it asks of you.
  • Nothing is announced. A world is here when it has emerged.
